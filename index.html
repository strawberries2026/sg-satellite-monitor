♡ Satellite advisory

Understand what the satellite predictions mean before using the map.

Predicted positions, not live tracking

Positions, altitude and speed are calculated from published orbital elements. They are not live GPS, satellite camera feeds or verified observations.

Visibility over Singapore

Above the horizon means a calculated elevation above 0°. It does not guarantee a visible pass; daylight, brightness, clouds and viewing conditions matter.

Military and science mission limitations

Public catalogues are incomplete. Names or categories do not establish ownership, mission purpose, operational status or affiliation with NASA or another agency.

Data freshness and availability

Orbital elements may be delayed or unavailable. Positions are recalculated using cached elements, and accuracy can deteriorate as orbital data ages. Check Data Health Centre for current freshness.

Safety and permitted use

This experimental reference tool must not be used for aviation, maritime, navigation, security, emergency response or other operational decisions. Consult authoritative sources when accuracy matters.
